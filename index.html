No Thanks!

The card game of clever refusals

How to Play

  • Cards numbered 3–35 are shuffled; 9 are removed unseen.
  • On your turn, either take the card (and all chips on it) or pay 1 chip to pass.
  • If you have no chips, you must take the card.
  • Runs of consecutive cards only count the lowest value.
  • Each chip is worth −1 point. Lowest score wins!
